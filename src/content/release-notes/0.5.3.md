## A calmer look

Kusema's window has a quieter, more professional look. It's mostly shades of
grey now, in both dark and light mode, so your attention goes to your
settings and not the decoration. The purple brand colour shows only where it
means something: a switch that's on, the ring around whatever has keyboard
focus, links, and progress.

- **New icons.** Every icon in the window is now one simple line style, from
  the open-source Iconoir set.
- **Clearer pages.** Each group of settings sits on its own panel with thin
  dividers instead of boxes inside boxes, and the text is easier to read.
- **Simpler buttons.** The main button on each screen is plain white (black in
  light mode), other buttons are quiet, and buttons that delete things are a
  soft red.
- **A title bar that matches.** On Windows 11 the title bar takes the app's own
  colour, so the window looks like one piece.
- **Easier to read.** Every piece of text and every control has enough
  contrast to read comfortably, in both modes. The sidebar can now be reached
  with the keyboard, with a clear outline around the item you're on.

The recording pill and the glow around your screen look exactly as before.

## "AI Provider"

The page where you choose your AI service and enter its key was called "Post
Process". It's now called **AI Provider**, and the buttons that lead to it say
so too. Nothing about how it works has changed.

## Amazon Bedrock, finished

The Amazon Bedrock provider (until now "AWS Bedrock (Mantle)") is complete. Use AI models in your firm's own AWS account, with Claude among them.

- **Claude works.** Claude models on Bedrock answer through Anthropic's own API on Bedrock's endpoint; other Bedrock models work as before.
- **Choose your region.** Pick one of the regions where Bedrock's endpoint is available (US East (N. Virginia) unless you change it). AWS GovCloud regions are under **Other regions** and need a GovCloud account.
- **The models your region offers.** The model list comes from the region you chose, without models AWS marks as unavailable, grouped by developer with Claude first. Kusema suggests a Claude model when the region lists one: its preferred Claude models first, otherwise the newest Claude listed.
- **Models from other developers** such as DeepSeek and Qwen run in AWS-operated accounts the developer can't access, and AWS states it doesn't share your content with them.
- **Test connection** checks your key, region and model in one go, and says plainly what's wrong: a key that has expired (keys from the console last up to 12 hours), a key from a different region, a model your account can't use there, or a key Bedrock doesn't accept.
- **Screen questions** with a model that can't see images now say so, instead of a general error.
- **Privacy:** your text goes to your own AWS account, in the region you choose, under your AWS agreement. AWS states it doesn't share your content with model providers, but may keep it for safety and abuse checks. Depending on the model, AWS may process it in other regions. Requests show up on the Privacy page with the region in the address, and Privileged mode blocks them like any cloud AI.
- **Web search** with Bedrock uses Brave Search or a SearXNG server, since Claude's own search isn't available on Bedrock.

Claude models on Bedrock require Anthropic model access in your AWS account. In commercial regions it's enabled on first use for most accounts; if Bedrock says a Claude model isn't available for your account, AWS has to enable it for you (in AWS GovCloud: Bedrock console → Bedrock configurations → Model access).

Your existing Bedrock key and model are kept. For IT: setting up model access, API keys, permissions, budgets and logging is described in the IT administration guide.

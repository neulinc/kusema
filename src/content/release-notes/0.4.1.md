## You always know when it's listening

While Kusema is listening, a colored glow appears around the edges of the screen
you're working on, easy to see from across the room.

- **A color for each mode:** green for dictation, red for continuous dictation,
  blue for Ask Kusema and purple for Ask about my screen. When continuous
  dictation pauses because you changed windows, the glow turns amber and dashed.
- **It stays out of your way.** You can click and type through it, and it never
  takes focus. Only you see it: it stays out of screenshots and screen shares.
- **Make it yours:** under **Recording indicator** on the General page, you can
  turn it off, change its thickness and intensity, or choose color-blind friendly
  colors. If Windows animations are off, it doesn't pulse.
- **Sounds:** continuous dictation now plays a short sound when it starts and
  stops. You can turn this off in the same place.

## A cleaner Assistant page

The Assistant page now has two tabs. **Conversation** opens first, with your
newest message in view and **New conversation** at the top. **Settings** holds the
AI provider, voice and screen question settings. Kusema remembers the tab you used
last.

## Kusema's own links and credits

- The About page now links to Kusema's source code, **Kusema for organizations**
  and a place to **report a problem or suggest a feature**. The donation button is
  gone.
- **Credits and licenses** on the About page says what Kusema is built on: Handy by
  CJ Pais, and every open-source component and speech or voice model, with its
  license.
- Help links that went to Handy's website now go to Kusema's.

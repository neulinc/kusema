## See your words as you speak

Hold Right Ctrl and talk: your words now show in the pill as you say them, the
last line or two, like Dragon's results box. The same happens in continuous
dictation. Punctuation you say shows as it will be typed ("comma" as a comma, a
new paragraph as ¶).

It's a preview. What's typed into your document is still worked out from
everything you said when you let go (or pause, in continuous dictation), so
it's as accurate as before, and the preview itself is never typed.

The preview runs on your computer and needs a model that can show live text:
Parakeet Unified, the default, or Nemotron Streaming. If your computer can't
keep up, Kusema updates it less often, and if that's still too much it turns
the preview off and tells you once. You can turn it on or off under
**General → Kusema Key → Live preview**.

## Check your microphone

A new microphone check, in setup and under **General → Sound**: stay quiet for
three seconds, then read one sentence aloud. Kusema tells you in plain words
what would help, for example:

- "Your room is a bit noisy. A headset will improve accuracy and stop stray
  words."
- "Your microphone is quiet. Move closer or raise its level in Windows sound
  settings."
- "This Bluetooth headset sends lower-quality audio while its mic is in use."

Any USB microphone or headset works; the check lists what to look for if
you're getting one. Press **Play my test back** to hear how you sound. The
test isn't saved or sent anywhere.

## Fewer stray words

After the check, Kusema knows how loud your room is and ignores anything
quieter than that, such as a television or people talking nearby, so they no
longer turn into stray words in your document. In our test with someone else
talking in the room, that went from 44 stray words to none, without losing any
of yours. It's on by default (**Ignore room noise**, under **General → Sound**).

## Ask about my screen

Ask Kusema about the window you're working in. Hold **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Space**
and ask something like "What do you think of this document?" or "What does
this error mean?"

- **Only that window** is captured, at the moment you press the shortcut. A
  brief border shows what was captured.
- **Nothing leaves your computer until you say so.** A card shows the
  screenshot, your question and where it will go. Press Send (or Enter) to
  send it, or Cancel (or Escape). If you don't answer, it cancels after 30
  seconds.
- **Follow-up questions** about the same screenshot work with the normal Ask
  Kusema shortcut, like "Make the first paragraph shorter."
- **Keep confidential documents local:** on the Assistant page you can send
  screen questions to a local vision model in Ollama while other questions
  still go to your usual provider.

It needs a model that can see images, such as Claude; Kusema tells you if the
one you chose can't. You can change the shortcut, and where screenshots go,
under **Ask About My Screen** on the Assistant page.

Screenshots are kept in memory only. They're never saved to disk, and History
only notes that a question had one. They're gone when you start a new
conversation or quit Kusema. Windows only for now.

## Kusema tells you when it can't answer

Before, if the Assistant wasn't set up, asking just closed the pill and
nothing happened. Now Kusema says why, in the pill, for a few seconds:

- "Kusema needs an AI to answer. Set one up in Assistant → Settings."
- "No API key for Anthropic."
- "Ollama isn't running on this computer. Start it, then ask again."
- "Privileged mode is on, so cloud AI is blocked."

Press **Set up** to go straight to the setting that fixes it, or **×** to close
the message. If you have spoken replies on, Kusema reads it out too. This works
however you ask: tapping Space while holding the key, starting with "Kusema",
typing in the Ask box, or asking about your screen. If you tap Space and then
don't say anything, Kusema tells you it didn't hear a question instead of
closing without a word.

## Labels no longer cover the pill

"Ask Kusema", "Editing", the name of the style shaping your text, and
Privileged now sit on their own line above the pill, instead of on top of the
sound wave. The pill grows smoothly to make room (no animation if you've asked
Windows for less motion), and the wave stays exactly where it was. Long names
wrap onto a second line. If your pill is at the top of the screen, the line
opens below it.

## Tap T to type a question

Hold Right Ctrl to talk. Tap Space while holding to ask by voice, or **T** to
type.

Tapping T while you hold the Kusema key opens the small Ask box over your
window. Whatever you'd started saying is dropped, and the T isn't typed into
your document. **Type to Kusema** in the tray still works too.

Ctrl+Alt+K is now one of the classic shortcuts, off unless you turn on
**Classic Shortcuts** under **General → Kusema Key**. If you had changed it to
another key, you keep your key.

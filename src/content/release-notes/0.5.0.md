## One key for everything

Kusema now has one key instead of a shortcut for every action. Two rules are all
there is:

- **Hold Right Ctrl to talk.** Let go, and your words are typed. **Double-tap**
  it for continuous dictation, and double-tap again to stop.
- **Say "Kusema" first** to ask, edit or look at your screen instead: "Kusema,
  what's the capital of Ghana?", "Kusema, make this more formal", "Kusema, what's
  on my screen?". Anything else you say is typed, even if it mentions Kusema
  later on.

Kusema keeps the key to itself, so it never does anything in your other apps.
No Right Ctrl on your laptop? Choose the **Copilot key**, **Numpad +** (if you're
used to Dragon) or press any spare key, under **General → Kusema Key**. AI
cleanup follows its setting, so there's no separate cleanup key. **Esc** still
cancels.

Like the old way? Turn on **Classic Shortcuts** to keep Ctrl+Space and the rest.
If you had changed one of them, they're kept for you, and a card on the General
page explains what's new.

## Edit by voice

Change text in any app without touching the keyboard. Select it, hold the
Kusema key and say "Kusema," then what you want: "make this more formal",
"shorten to two sentences", "turn this into bullet points", "fix the grammar" or
"translate to Spanish". The same works during continuous dictation.

- **Edit or question?** "Make", "shorten", "rewrite", "translate", "fix" and
  the like change the text. "What does this mean?" or "explain this" asks about
  the selected text instead. The pill shows which one Kusema chose.
- **You see it first.** A card shows the text now and the new version. Press
  **Enter** to replace it or **Esc** to cancel. Changed your mind afterwards?
  **Ctrl+Z** in the app undoes it. For quick edits, you can turn the preview off
  on the General page.
- **Only what you asked.** The AI changes only what you ask for and adds no facts.
  Your text is sent as text to edit, never as instructions, so a document that
  says "ignore your instructions" can't take over.
- **Nothing selected?** Just dictate, then say how to change it: Kusema uses the
  text it typed a moment ago, if it can find exactly that text again. Otherwise
  it asks you to select it.
- **Private when it needs to be.** In Privileged mode only a model on your own
  computer is used, and every voice edit is listed in the privacy ledger, with its
  size only.

## Move and select by voice

Say "Kusema," and a command, like "Kusema, select last word". These run on your
computer and send nothing anywhere:

- "select last word", "select last three words", "select line", "select all"
- "select last sentence", "select last paragraph", or "select" and the words you
  want, like "select the motion"
- "go to start of line", "go to end of document" and the like
- "delete that", "cut that", "copy that", "paste that", "undo that"

Most of them also work during continuous dictation, said on their own. To see
them all, open **General → Voice Editing → What can I say?**

The commands are in English. Selecting a sentence, paragraph or phrase needs an
app that lets Kusema read its text, such as Word, Outlook, Notepad or a text box
in Chrome or Edge. The other commands work almost everywhere.

Some apps, such as ONLYOFFICE, LibreOffice and Google Docs, draw their own text.
There, select the text with the mouse or keyboard first: Kusema copies it, puts
your clipboard back afterwards, and replaces it as usual.

## Clear messages

- **You always know what happened.** If an edit can't be done, the pill says
  why, for example "Couldn't read the selected text in this app" or that
  Privileged mode blocked it. Nothing fails silently.
- **"Select last word"** now takes the whole word with its punctuation, even when
  it's the only word on the line.

## Kusema learns your words

- **Legal terms out of the box:** Latin and legal terms like "habeas corpus",
  "voir dire" and "res judicata" are written correctly, and so are citations:
  "42 U.S.C.", "123 F.3d 456", "Smith v. Jones", "Rule 12(b)(6)". On by default
  for English; turn it off on the new **Vocabulary** page.
- **Medical terms, if you need them:** a pack of drug names and clinical terms,
  off unless you turn it on (in setup or on the Vocabulary page). Always review
  dictated medical records.
- **Your own words:** add names and terms, and how Kusema tends to mishear them.
  Import and export word lists to share with colleagues, or bring yours over from
  Dragon.
- **"Correct that":** when Kusema gets a word wrong, say "Kusema, correct that",
  fix it in the box that opens and press Enter. Kusema replaces it in your
  document and remembers the word for next time. You can also correct from
  History, or from **Correct Last Dictation** in the tray. What Kusema learns
  stays on your computer and is listed on the Vocabulary page, where you can
  remove it.

## Type to Kusema

When you can't talk, type. Press **Ctrl+Alt+K**, or choose **Type to Kusema** in
the tray, and a small **Ask Kusema** box opens over the window you're working in.
Type your question, press **Enter**, and the answer appears right there. **Esc**
closes it and puts you back where you were. There's also a box at the bottom of
the Assistant's **Conversation** tab.

- **The same Kusema:** typed and spoken questions share one conversation, the
  same AI provider, Privileged mode and privacy ledger.
- **Quiet by default:** answers to typed questions aren't read aloud. Turn
  **Speak Answers to Typed Questions** on if you want them to be.
- **About your screen, too:** the screen button in the box asks about the window
  behind it, with the same preview before anything is sent.

## Answers straight into your document

Say or type "draft a reply declining this meeting **and type it**", or end with
"…put it in the document", and Kusema types the answer where your cursor was,
ready to use: no "Sure, here's a draft". To do this for every answer, set
**Answer goes to** to **Type into document** (or **Both**) on the Assistant page.

- **Only where you asked.** The answer goes into the window you asked from and
  nowhere else. If you've clicked away, Kusema waits for you to come back.
- **Easy to undo.** One **Ctrl+Z** takes it out again.
- **Insert it later:** every answer has **Insert into document**.

## Tutor style

For students: turn on **Tutor Style**, or say "explain step by step" in any
question. Kusema explains one step at a time, checks you've followed, and helps
you reach the answer instead of handing over homework answers, unless you ask
for the answer. Follow your school's or exam's rules on AI use.

## A style for each app

AI cleanup, AI voice edits and answers typed into a document now match the
program they go into: email format in Outlook, formal in Word and ONLYOFFICE,
short and casual in Teams and Slack, and neutral everywhere else. The pill shows
the style while it's working. Change the styles in plain words, or add your own
programs, under **Assistant → Settings → Styles per App**. Kusema only looks at the
program's name, on your computer. The quick cleanup that runs on your computer
isn't affected.

## A new recording pill

- **It glows with your voice:** a soft, slowly moving glow in indigo, violet and
  a touch of amber, with a live waveform inside the pill.
- **You can tell what it's doing at a glance:** listening, thinking, speaking
  and done each look different. Paused and problems have their own icon and
  edge, so you don't have to rely on colour. An icon shows which mode is
  listening.
- **Calm if you prefer:** with Windows animations turned off, the glow stays
  still.
- **Just for you:** the pill no longer shows in screenshots or screen shares,
  like the screen glow.
- **Less glow for quick dictation:** the glow around the screen is now off for
  normal dictation and stays on for continuous dictation and questions. Turn it
  back on with **Glow for Normal Dictation**.

## Continuous dictation

Dictate a long letter or file note without holding a key. Press
**Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D** and keep talking: each time you pause, your words are typed
into your document. Press it again to stop. Coming from Dragon? One click under
**Continuous Dictation** on the General page makes it **Numpad +**.

- **Voice commands:** say "new paragraph", "new line", "scratch that" or "stop
  dictation" on its own, between pauses.
- **It stays in your document.** If you switch windows, typing pauses until you
  come back. What you say while you're away is saved in History, not typed.
- **Nothing is lost.** Your words are saved to History as you go. The audio isn't
  kept.
- **Never left on by accident.** The overlay shows that you're dictating, with a
  Stop button, and dictation stops by itself after 3 minutes of silence.

Windows only for now.

## Spoken punctuation

Say "comma", "period", "question mark", "colon", "open quote", "close quote",
"section symbol" and more, as you would in Dragon. Normal sentences keep their
words: "the limitations period expired" stays as it is. Say "literal period" to
type the word itself.

Kusema also adds a missing period between sentences in continuous dictation. All
of this happens on your computer, and you can turn it off in the new **Cleanup**
section on the General page. AI cleanup is there too: it's off unless you turn it
on, and it's told to fix your wording without adding facts.

## See what leaves your computer

- **The Privacy page** lists every request Kusema makes: when, where, why and how
  large, but never what was in it. Speech recognition runs on your computer, so
  no audio is sent anywhere. Kusema sends no telemetry or analytics.
- **Privileged mode** stops anything going to a cloud AI service, with one switch.
  Turn it on from the Privacy page, the Assistant page or the tray menu. Dictation
  keeps working, and Ask Kusema keeps working with an AI model on your computer,
  such as Ollama. A "Privileged" label and a tray badge show when it's on.

Dictated text also stays out of Windows' clipboard history and cloud clipboard.

## Easier setup

New installs go from opening Kusema to a first dictated sentence in about two
minutes: choose your language, check your microphone, practice on screen, and
choose how the AI assistant works or skip it. The setup works by keyboard and with
large text.

Everyone gets a shorter **Models** page: four models, described in plain language.
The full list is under **Show all models** in Advanced.

## Changes to models

Models whose licenses don't clearly allow use in a paid product are no longer
offered, including some non-English Moonshine models. If you were using one,
Kusema selects another model you have, or asks you to choose one.

## Web search and link reading (off by default)

Kusema can now look things up on the web and read a page you name. It's off until you turn it on in **Assistant → Settings → Web search**.

- **Ask for it:** "search the web for this year's filing fees", "look up the court's holiday hours", "what's the latest on the Johnson merger?" or "news about remote notarization", or switch on **Search the web** beside the typed question box. Or choose **Automatic** and let the AI decide when a question needs the web.
- **Answered without searching?** A **Search the web for this** button under the answer asks the same question again with a search. After a spoken question, the pill shows how to ask for a search.
- **Read a page:** copy a link and ask "summarize this link", or say the address ("what does example.com/rules say about deadlines?"). Kusema reads only that page: public web pages only, never this computer or your office network, and it never follows links on the page.
- **Search with** your own Brave Search key, a SearXNG search server (your firm's own, if you have one), or Claude's or OpenAI's built-in search when that's your AI.
- **Sources** appear as links under each answer. Spoken answers don't read addresses aloud, and pasted answers end with a short sources list (you can turn that off).
- **Limits:** at most 3 searches per question and the top 5 results of each, as short snippets.

### Privacy

- **Nothing goes to the web unless you turn it on.** Then only search text goes to the search provider you chose, and only the page you named is read.
- **Confirm searches** (off by default) shows each search before it's sent, so you can change or cancel it.
- **Your screenshot or selected text stays out of searches.** When a question includes one, Kusema asks before searching, and the search is made from your words alone.
- **Web pages can't give Kusema orders.** Their text goes to the AI marked as information to use, never as instructions to follow.
- **Every search and page read is listed** on the Privacy page, with what was searched for or the address. That list is stored only on this computer, and Clear removes it with the rest.
- **Privileged mode blocks all web access**, and your IT administrator can turn web access off for the whole computer.

## New AI providers: DeepSeek and Google Gemini

**DeepSeek** and **Google Gemini** join Anthropic and OpenAI. The provider list now starts with the AIs on your computer (Ollama, LM Studio), then Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google Gemini and OpenAI.

Each cloud provider shows a one-line note on what it does with your text, with a **Details** link to its terms:

- **Anthropic** and **OpenAI** don't train on API data by default. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days; OpenAI keeps it up to 30 days to watch for abuse.
- **DeepSeek** processes your text on servers in China. DeepSeek's policy allows it to use data to improve its services; see its terms.
- **Google Gemini:** on the free tier, Google may use your prompts and answers to improve its products, and people may read them. Use a paid key for confidential work.

**Test key** checks your key and lists the provider's current models, and picks a recommended one if you haven't chosen yet. If you reached OpenAI through the Custom provider, Kusema moves you to the OpenAI provider, with your key and model.
